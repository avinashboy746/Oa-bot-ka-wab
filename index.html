Depending on enabled features, OA BOT may process:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Channel and Role IDs.
  • Server configuration settings.
  • Moderation logs, if enabled.
  • Command information needed to provide features.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate commands, moderation, server settings, security features, and improve the reliability of OA BOT.

Information may be stored using the hosting and database services used by OA BOT. Reasonable security measures should be maintained.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot or disclosed where required by applicable law.

Users may contact us to request deletion of eligible stored information. We may request relevant Discord IDs to identify the data.

7. User Choices

Server administrators may remove OA BOT from their Discord server and disable optional features.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may change when our services or data practices change. Updated documents will be published on this website.
